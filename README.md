# N's Win Streaks

A personal life planner that works as an app on your phone: plan across eight areas of life, tick things off, and see your daily and weekly wins.

**Open it:** https://artxpdev.github.io/win-streaks/ — then in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

## Privacy
- Your entries, to-dos and settings are saved only in the browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to GitHub or any server.
- Google Calendar sync signs in with your own Google account and talks straight to Google from your device.
- Use **Record → Export backup** now and then to keep a copy of your data.
